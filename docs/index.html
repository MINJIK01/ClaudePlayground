About this graph

Recent arXiv papers on persona and memory, linked by textual similarity

Papers are collected every weekday from cs.CL, cs.LG and cs.AI when their title or abstract contains a tracked keyword. Each paper is linked to the papers whose title and abstract are most similar to it (TF-IDF cosine similarity).

  1. Select a paper to read its abstract and see its closest papers.
  2. Change Links per paper to show more or fewer connections.
  3. Select a keyword chip to show or hide that group.

Similarity reflects shared vocabulary, so two papers that describe the same idea in different words may not be linked.
